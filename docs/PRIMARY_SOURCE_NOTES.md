# Primary source verification notes

Sources were checked on 9 September 2026. These notes distinguish reported observations from inherited assumptions.

- Official Eliquis label and Health Canada review: approximately 50% oral bioavailability; 87% protein binding; systemic clearance 3.3 L/h and renal contribution 0.9 L/h. These are not apparent oral clearances to be multiplied by F. https://packageinserts.bms.com/pi/pi_eliquis.pdf and https://dhpp.hpfb-dgpsa.ca/review-documents/resource/SBD00050/
- Luettgen et al. 2011, DOI 10.3109/14756366.2010.535793: free Xa Ki 0.25 nM at 37 C, compared with 0.075 nM at 25 C. Prothrombinase mixed-inhibition constants are Ki,E 0.62 and Ki,ES 1.68 nM. The approximately 150-nM substrate constant is not reproduced by the inherited model's approximately 14,075-nM coefficient ratio. Exact new reaction mapping is a stated approximation. https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/pdf/10.3109/14756366.2010.535793
- Hockin et al. 2002, DOI 10.1074/jbc.M201173200: the original published coagulation model is structurally different from the repository's reduced cascade. The repository's rate constants are not claimed to be a faithful transcription. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11893748/
- Frost et al. 2013, DOI 10.1111/bcp.12106: Table 2 day-7 5-mg BID cohort (n=6) supplies Cmax 128.5, Cmin 49.6 ng/mL, AUCtau 1051.9 ng.h/mL, and median Tmax 4 h with range 2-4. The range is not a confidence interval. https://bpspubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/bcp.12106
- Kelddal et al. 2025, DOI 10.1016/j.xkme.2025.101136: n=11 NS and n=10 healthy. Steady-state apixaban means 35 and 51 ng/mL; ETP means 1096 and 910 nM.min. The full-text results report NS ETP CI 868-1324 and P=0.18, whereas the abstract reports an upper CI of 1355 and P=0.19. The result table uses the full-text results interval and does not use either P value for model qualification. The article also has inconsistent concentration and ETP unit typography; the methods/assay scale control unit interpretation. TF reagent concentration was not verified. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12666546/
- Derebail et al. 2023, DOI 10.1053/j.ajkd.2022.09.011: single-dose phase 1a study motivates total/free PK measurements. A nonrenal intrinsic-clearance reduction was not independently identified. The revised primary disease multiplier is one; 0.82 and 0.70 are sensitivity hypotheses, not fitted patient-level estimates. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36328100/
- Besser et al. 2008, DOI 10.1111/j.1538-7836.2008.03117.x: recurrent venous thrombosis, not an AF stroke-risk transfer function. The inappropriate stroke mapping is disabled. Platelet-reactivity literature likewise does not calibrate the model's arbitrary HC integral to bleeding hazard.

No new patient-level data were obtained. Disease gradients, platelet coefficients, tissue partition assumptions, age effects, and feedback strengths remain provisional. Source disagreements were not resolved by inventing observations or fitting the model to a preferred answer.
