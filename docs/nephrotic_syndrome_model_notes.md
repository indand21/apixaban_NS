# Scientific scope of the revised NS model

Full methods and equations are described in the associated manuscript and its supplement (not included in this repository). The authoritative numeric outputs are in output/revised, regenerated by running this repository's pipeline (see README.md).

## Binding and clearance

Nominal systemic references are 3.3 L/h total and 0.9 L/h renal. The oral dose is multiplied by 0.50 once. Intrinsic coefficients are derived by dividing local total-drug coefficients by reference fu=0.13. Elimination acts on unbound tissue-return concentration; protein-associated loss acts on the bound fraction. Partition coefficients scale with fu under an unchanged-tissue-binding assumption. This finite-flow, portal-input model only approximately follows the low-extraction unbound AUC identity. Half-life need not remain invariant and is tested against exact linear-system eigenvalues.

Primary nonrenal intrinsic-clearance disease multipliers are all one. Values 0.82 and 0.70 are sensitivity hypotheses, not directly identified clinical estimates. Bound loss coefficients of 0/0.005/0.01/0.02 L/h are illustrative. Plasma exposure alone cannot uniquely identify all elimination, binding, and bioavailability mechanisms. The old calibration to an uncertain small-study point estimate combined with 0.20 L/h bound loss is not retained as an established physiological result.

## Coagulation and assay

The 34-species model differs from the published Hockin system. All 40 rate entries are loaded from CSV with unit conversion; unused k17 and k42 were removed. Inherited coefficients are explicitly provisional. Free Xa Ki=0.25 nM and prothrombinase Ki,E=0.62/Ki,ES=1.68 nM are from Luettgen et al. at 37 C, using a rapid-equilibrium approximation. Their inclusion does not reproduce the full purified-enzyme kinetic experiment.

Primary TF is fixed at 5 pM. Protein C activation is disabled without a represented thrombomodulin condition; the legacy effective activation term is a sensitivity condition. ETP can remain nearly invariant when most prothrombin is eventually converted and thrombin is terminated by antithrombin. A short assay window can mistake delayed generation for suppressed total generation. Full curves, peak, and timing must accompany ETP.

## Platelets and dose interpretation

Platelet pools, the desensitized sink, and accumulated surface signal are phenomenological. HC is a model-state integral, not a molar platelet concentration or validated bleeding measure. Feedback coefficients are assumptions, not calibrated dose bounds. Both composite-score denominators use the same individual and feedback mode without drug.

Synthetic individuals use declared independent design distributions and common draws across disease scenarios. They are not a calibrated NS population. TF uncertainty is prior propagation without posterior updating. Mathematical boundary maxima and weak scores do not support prescribing nomograms. The external comparisons retain the model's exposure and ETP mismatches, and no clinical event-rate mapping is used.
